# Crave Hunt Research Library

This directory is the canonical internal research layer for Crave Hunt Patna.

Every record should preserve provenance. A recommendation is not automatically a fact. Community posts are signals; official sources and multiple independent confirmations receive stronger confidence.

## Record families
- places: stalls, restaurants, markets, food streets, legacy institutions
- dishes: dish identity, variants, ingredients, cultural context
- areas: neighbourhoods, food corridors, markets
- people: cooks, founders, food writers, creators, historians
- media: Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, articles, maps
- events: food festivals, fairs, pop-ups, food walks
- history: dated culinary/cultural milestones
- contexts: season, occasion, ritual role and availability scope
- hunts: community requests awaiting investigation

## Evidence rules
- Cultural context is not the same thing as current Patna availability.
- Event food is time-bounded unless separately corroborated as permanent.
- Creator and follower metrics are dated observations, not authority scores.
- Multiple articles repeating the same upstream claim do not count as independent corroboration.
- Community and creator sources are valuable discovery signals but require appropriate confidence and provenance.

## Research status
