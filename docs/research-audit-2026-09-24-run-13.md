# Crave Hunt Patna Research Audit — Run 13

Date: 2026-09-24

## Research shift

This run shifted away from broad discovery toward freshness delta, operational-state contradictions, creator provenance, and the conversion of short-form food media into geographic entities.

## Material findings

1. Saras Mela remains active through 2026-09-29. JEEViKA reporting cited by Patna Press records more than ₹68.56 lakh cumulative business in the first three days, including more than ₹25.37 lakh on day three. These figures are dated reported observations, not permanent event attributes.
2. Chaat Guru on Exhibition Road remains a useful state-conflict test case. The current Magicpin page contains both a shutdown notice and an Open now label. Crave Hunt should surface this as contradictory evidence rather than choose one silently.
3. @urgeforfood and @happiness_on_my_plate surfaced as current Patna creator research leads. Third-party follower and engagement estimates are stored as observations and are not treated as platform verified metrics.
4. Current third-party listings surfaced Pappu Chaiwalla near Gandhi Maidan and Street Foods by Punjab Grill at PNM Mall, Patliputra Industrial Area. These are place leads requiring canonical entity matching before publication.
5. Waybites provides a strong comparable product pattern: creator videos are transformed into mappable food spots. This directly supports a Crave Hunt relationship between creator media and place entities.
6. Bihar Foundation remains a high-confidence culinary reference and should continue to anchor canonical dish identity separately from Patna venue availability.

## Data quality decisions

- No duplicate place entity was created from the new venue leads.
- Creator metrics are temporal observations, not static fields.
- Event sales are temporal observations with reported status.
- Conflicting operating labels remain unresolved until stronger evidence appears.

## Product implication

The corpus is now sufficiently mature that additional generic restaurant discovery has diminishing value. The higher-value primitive is an evidence-backed relationship graph where creator media can point to dishes and places, and every volatile claim has a date and provenance.

## Next build target

Implement public evidence timelines and creator-to-place media relationships across entity pages. The UI should distinguish verified facts, reported observations, community signals, and unresolved contradictions.
