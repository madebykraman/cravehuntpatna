7. Current Saras Mela reporting shows the event remains active through September 29, 2026; one current report records more than 135 stalls and Rs 68.56 lakh in business during the first three days. This remains event reporting, not audited transaction data.

## Research strategy adjustment

The search emphasis shifted from adding restaurant names to detecting changes in Patna's food infrastructure. Search terms now distinguish organic food corridors, organised vending zones, proposed food hubs, operational food hubs, and heritage food contexts. This reduces the risk of treating municipal plans as current dining reality.

## Contradictions / uncertainty retained

- Exhibition Road density is supported by editorial sources but requires stronger independent corroboration.
- Creator follower and engagement values can differ between analytics providers and change rapidly; preserve observations with timestamps rather than overwrite history.
- Proposed vending zones must not be presented as operational without current evidence.
- Bihar-wide heritage dishes are separate from Patna-local claims unless a Patna-specific source establishes the connection.

## Next highest-value build

Build the Locality Atlas UI against the research corpus. Each locality should expose corridor type, lifecycle status, dishes, places, creators, events, evidence count, freshness, pincode confidence, and unresolved claims. The interface should distinguish verified facts from community/editorial signals rather than collapsing them into a single rating.
