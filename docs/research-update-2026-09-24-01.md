# Crave Hunt Research Update — 2026-09-24

## New verified research signals

- District Patna's official history establishes the Pataliputra/Magadh historical layer, Chandragupta Maurya's capital, and later Patna revival under Sher Shah Suri.
- Bihar Tourism's official material describes Bihar cuisine as seasonal and ingredient-led, with rice, mustard oil and dairy as important elements.
- Government of Bihar cuisine material reinforces Bihar's distinct regional food culture.
- Recent Reddit threads add contemporary dish/place signals: Mings Chimney for momo; Basant Vihar for dosa; Chourasiya Ji Ki Purani Litti Dukan and Boring Road stalls for litti; Pista House for biryani/kebabs; Santushti Sweets for cutlet; Champaran Meat House for mutton. These remain community leads, not editorial verdicts.
- A Reddit thread explicitly searching for Patna food creators identifies Garda Patna Wale and WhatPatnaeats as possible Instagram research leads; direct verification is still required.
- Current event inventory now includes Saras Mela at Gyan Bhawan and a Navratri/Dandiya event with a food court; event data should remain link-first and time-sensitive.

## Research metric changes

1. Increase weight on primary/official historical sources for heritage claims.
2. Require multiple independent community mentions before upgrading a place from lead to verified.
3. Separate place-level claims from dish-level claims; do not infer a place's signature dish from one comment.
4. Track creator/media accounts as leads until canonical profile URLs are verified.
5. Search by locality + dish + landmark, not only restaurant name, to uncover informal stalls.
6. Add temporal decay: recent community evidence should influence freshness, but older historical evidence remains valid for heritage claims.
7. Track contradictions explicitly rather than averaging them into a single rating.
