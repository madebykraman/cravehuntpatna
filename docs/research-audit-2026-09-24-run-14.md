# Crave Hunt Patna — Research Audit — Run 14

Date: 2026-09-24

## Strategy change
This run moved away from another broad venue sweep. The primary research metric was infrastructure and state transition: planned food hubs, corridor redesigns, event food intensity, and evidence independence. A source-quality penalty was also applied to unsupported editorial superlatives.

## Material findings

1. Patna municipal food infrastructure is becoming a first-class research layer. A reported proposal identifies three possible dedicated food hubs: JP Ganga Path near Digha Golambar, Khatal Gali in Kotwali, and the common service centre premises in Mithapur. This remains a reported plan, not an operational fact.

2. Digha Ganga Path has multiple reported design phases. An earlier vending-stall model was later reported as being replaced by a proposal for about 85 branded food cafes near Digha roundabout. These must remain dated plan observations rather than one canonical corridor state.

3. Saras Mela remains a useful temporal event dataset. Current reporting says the 2026 edition runs September 20-29 at Gyan Bhawan with 135+ stalls. A September 23 report gives more than Rs 68.56 lakh in business during the first three days. The figure is stored as a dated reported observation, not an audited metric.

4. A current Exhibition Road guide provides useful dish/stall discovery leads but uses strong claims such as 'densest street food zone' without transparent methodology. It is therefore retained as a discovery lead and does not raise corridor confidence by itself.

5. Streatzo confirms a current Indian product pattern around map-based street-food discovery, vendors, pop-ups, home chefs, creator stories and engagement. Crave Hunt should not compete by becoming another generic map directory; its differentiator remains evidence-backed locality intelligence and historical context.

## Deduplication
No duplicate canonical Saras Mela, Digha corridor, Pappu Chaiwalla or Street Foods by Punjab Grill entities were created. New material is attached as observations or research leads.

## Contradictions
The main contradiction is temporal rather than factual: Digha food infrastructure has been reported under more than one design. The archive preserves those plans separately instead of presenting the latest discovered plan as timeless truth.

## Product implication
The current data model needs a stronger distinction between an entity's identity and its changing plans/state. A vending zone can be proposed, redesigned, delayed, operational, altered or closed while retaining historical evidence from each phase.

## Next highest-value build
Add `plan_version` and lifecycle observations to `food_street` and `vending_zone` entities, then render a dated evidence timeline on locality and corridor pages. This is higher value than adding another generic restaurant batch because it makes Crave Hunt's archive materially more defensible.
