# Crave Hunt Patna Research Audit · Run 04

Date: 2026-09-24

## What changed

Added source-backed discovery records for Exhibition Road and Fraser Road as food-corridor candidates, Bihar heritage dishes from Bihar Tourism, a new Patna creator lead (@adityacooks), and organised street-food infrastructure proposals covering Digha Golambar, Khatal Gali and Mithapur.

The discovery feed now records source freshness and source role for new entries. Secondary editorial guides remain discovery evidence rather than authoritative vendor rankings. Creator-index metrics remain distribution snapshots, not quality scores.

## Research signals

1. Exhibition Road appears as a high-density street-food research zone in a 2026 local guide. Vendor-level claims remain unverified.
2. Fraser Road appears as a central mixed corridor with historical-food claims that need independent archival corroboration before being presented as fact.
3. Bihar Tourism provides stronger primary-source culinary records for Khichdi, Litti Chokha, Champaran Meat, Pittha and Tilkut. These should become the backbone of the heritage dish layer.
4. Patna food geography is being reshaped by vending-zone and food-hub proposals. Digha/Ganga Path should therefore be modelled as changing infrastructure rather than a static restaurant list.
5. @adityacooks is a useful creator discovery lead because the indexed profile explicitly describes a Patna base and a Bihar-recipe focus. Reach metrics are not used as editorial quality signals.

## Contradictions / caution

Do not convert claims such as "oldest", "best", "densest", "most famous" or vendor-level recommendations from secondary guides into objective facts without independent corroboration.

The Ganga Path food-cafe proposal and the three proposed food hubs are infrastructure signals, not proof that the proposed outlets or zones are currently operational.

Saras Mela visitor and sales figures found in secondary reporting are event-performance claims and should remain reported figures until an official source is available.

## Search strategy change

This run shifted from broad restaurant discovery toward corridor-level geography, primary culinary documentation, creator identity discovery and municipal food-infrastructure signals. The next pass should move from corridor discovery to entity resolution: canonical locality, pincode, exact venue, dish, creator, source and temporal status relationships.

## Next highest-value build

Build the Patna Locality Atlas as a real data-driven surface. Each locality should expose its food corridors, dishes, places, creator coverage, events, evidence density, unresolved claims and freshness. This will provide the geographic spine for search, maps, Crave Hunts and future field research.
